    Excellence (Outtake)

    In an era where the average is available virtually for free, excellence becomes the final justification.

    Instruction

    # Excellence

## Arrangement

**Style:** Indie-Folk-Pop, Chamber-Folk  
**Voice:** Female, close-miking, breathy, ASMR quality, intimate.  

The core arrangement is driven by felt piano and a propulsive beat.

Crucial: The lyrics tell a story and must not be altered through omission, transposition, or duplication. Exploit the 3-minute performance window through dynamic instrumental interludes.

'cause good is never good enough, they want the sublime.

    Track Liner Notes

    Excellence did not survive onto the album, despite holding a locked position on the tracklist for an extended period. It was the second piece composed for the project, following directly after Hollow.

    The thematic premise confronted the existential condition of creative labor in the age of generative models: when the statistical median is available instantaneously and for free, excellence—whether measured by technical virtuosity or radical novelty—becomes the sole justification for creative survival.

    I supplied Gemini with the following lyrical parameters:

    The narrator is condemned to continuous, flawless execution: she is "excellent by demand." If she falters, she will be cast aside.

    In a world where mediocrity is commodified, excellence is the only hard currency.

    No one can truly define what excellence entails, but mere craft is insufficient—it must project genius and effortless innovation.

    Satisfy the crowd's expectations by delivering what they recognize, while disguising the fact that everything has already been done before.

    The model generated a draft that resonated immediately, which I submitted directly to Lyria for audio synthesis. The second iteration carried an infectious energy: a piano vamping pattern reminiscent of Fool's Garden's "Lemon Tree" paired with a driving, foot-tapping acoustic pulse.

    The irony of this take was historical: it was generated by Gemini 3.0 Flash, which was superseded by Gemini 3.5 a few weeks later. The model had unwittingly authored its own synthetic requiem—a poetic symmetry that fit the philosophical inquiry of the album.

    With critical distance, however, the flaws in the lyrics became obvious ("in a world of copper coins I'm the only currency"—an unearned, swaggering register break where my user profile context had bled into the text). I rewrote the draft extensively, adhering to my rule that no unedited machine text should appear on the record.

    Subsequent generations with the revised lyrics, however, completely failed to recapture the harmonic charm of the original take. Probability is stubborn: you never roll the same winning numbers twice. After five iterations, I halted production. More fundamentally, I realized the track was too extroverted and theatrical: it lacked interiority and narrative development, remaining a superficial complaint about professional demands. Its sonic palette leaned too heavily toward commercial musical theater, leading me to cut the piece from the album without replacement.
